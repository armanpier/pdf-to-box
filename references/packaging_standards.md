# Folding Carton Packaging Standards & Reference

## Dieline Terminology & Geometry
1. **Body Panels**:
   - Four primary vertical faces forming the tube of the folding carton:
     - **Back Panel**: Contains regulatory text, ingredients, barcode/recycling (unless on bottom), manufacturer address.
     - **Left Side Panel**: Secondary language / instructions / regulatory information.
     - **Front Panel**: Primary Display Panel (PDP) with hero branding, product title, key claims.
     - **Right Side Panel**: Additional language / usage instructions / decorative patterns.
2. **Glue Flap**:
   - Typically attached to the leftmost panel (Back panel) with tapered corners (~10 mm to 15 mm wide) that glues underneath the rightmost panel (Right panel) to close the sleeve.
3. **Top Closure**:
   - **Top Lid**: Hinged at the top fold line of the Front or Back panel.
   - **Top Tuck Flap**: Rounded tuck tongue (height ~13 mm to 15 mm) inserted inside the top of the box.
   - **Dust Flaps**: Hinged at the top of Left and Right panels, folded inward first to provide dust protection and support the lid.
4. **Bottom Closure**:
   - **Bottom Lid**: Hinged at the bottom fold line of the Front or Back panel.
   - **Bottom Tuck Flap**: Folded into the sleeve. In retail pharmacy and cosmetics, retail barcode is commonly printed directly on the Bottom Lid panel for easy scanning without cluttering the PDP.
   - **Bottom Dust Flaps**: Folded inward first.

## Cut vs. Crease Line Styles
- **Cuts (Solid Line, Standard Cyan / Spot Diecut)**: Outer perimeter defining paper cut edges, tuck tongues, slit locks, thumb notches.
- **Creases / Folds (Dashed or Distinct Color)**: Score lines where paperboard fibers are compressed to facilitate 90° or 180° folds.
